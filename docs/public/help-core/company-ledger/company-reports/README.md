# Company Reports

Company Reports summarizes the posted financial records of the company selected in the company switcher.

## Concepts

* [What Is a Financial Ledger?](../../../voyzu-core-concepts/what-is-a-financial-ledger.md) explains the records behind financial reports.
* [Financial Document Processing](../../../voyzu-core-concepts/financial-document-processing.md) explains how source documents become postings.
* [Control Accounts](../../../voyzu-core-concepts/control-accounts.md), [Inventory](../../../voyzu-core-concepts/inventory.md), and [Tax](../../../voyzu-core-concepts/tax.md) explain specialist ledger reporting.

## Statement and movement reports

Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Profit & Loss Analysis, Trial Balance, Account Activity, Bank / Cash Movement, and Journal Entries present general ledger position or movement.

## Tax and reconciliation reports

Tax Position, Tax Return, Tax Reconciliation, and Financial Integrity show tax results or agreement between supporting ledgers and the general ledger.

## Audit reports

The AP, AR, inventory, and tax ledger-entry reports provide printable entry-level extracts for a selected period.

## Using reports

Confirm the selected company and report scope before interpreting an output. Refresh after posting. Use the individual report's help page for its parameters, data, investigation path, and output options.
