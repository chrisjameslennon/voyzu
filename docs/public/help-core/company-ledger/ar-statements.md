# AR Statements

AR Statements assembles customer opening balance, movements, applications, and closing balance for enquiry.

## Concepts

* [What Is a Financial Ledger?](../../voyzu-core-concepts/what-is-a-financial-ledger.md) explains the AR subledger and immutable entries.
* [Financial Document Processing](../../voyzu-core-concepts/financial-document-processing.md) explains how customer documents post.
* [Control Accounts](../../voyzu-core-concepts/control-accounts.md) explains how AR entries connect to the general ledger.

## Selecting the data

Choose or search for the customer and set the available date or period scope. Confirm the selected company before reading the statement.

## Reading the detail

Read the opening balance first, then the dated invoices, credits, receipts, and applications that produce the closing balance. Follow an entry or document link when a movement needs explanation.

## Source and scope

A statement is a presentation of recorded AR activity. It does not create, edit, or settle customer entries.

## Refresh and export

Refresh before relying on recently posted activity. Export where the screen offers it, and record the selected company and filters with any output used as evidence.

## Corrections

This is an enquiry screen. Do not try to alter a ledger result here. Correct the source setup where appropriate, or post a supported correcting document or reversal so the financial and audit histories remain complete.
