# Tax Control Accounts

Tax Accounts shows how the selected company's tax ledger purposes point to general ledger accounts.

## Concepts

* [Tax](../../voyzu-core-concepts/tax.md) explains tax authorities, rules, components, filing, and posting.
* [What Is a Financial Ledger?](../../voyzu-core-concepts/what-is-a-financial-ledger.md) explains immutable ledger records.
* [Control Accounts](../../voyzu-core-concepts/control-accounts.md) explains the tax-to-GL mapping.

## Selecting the data

The screen lists tax purposes, linked GL accounts, required account types, and posting state. The effective mapping reflects the company's financial settings.

## Reading the detail

Use the mapping to understand where collected, paid, payable, receivable, and adjustment tax amounts appear in the GL. Country setup determines the tax rules; this page shows the posting destinations.

## Making changes

Open a control account and select an active GL account of the displayed type. **Tax on Sales** requires a Liability account; **Tax on Purchases** requires an Asset account. Posted mappings and organization-standard mappings are read-only.

## Refresh and export

Refresh before relying on recently posted activity. Export where the screen offers it, and record the selected company and filters with any output used as evidence.

## Corrections

This is an enquiry screen. Do not try to alter a ledger result here. Correct the source setup where appropriate, or post a supported correcting document or reversal so the financial and audit histories remain complete.
