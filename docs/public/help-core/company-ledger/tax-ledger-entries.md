# Tax Ledger Entries

Tax Ledger Entries shows tax amounts produced by the selected company's posted financial documents.

## Concepts

* [Tax](../../voyzu-core-concepts/tax.md) explains tax authorities, rules, components, filing, and posting.
* [What Is a Financial Ledger?](../../voyzu-core-concepts/what-is-a-financial-ledger.md) explains immutable ledger records.
* [Control Accounts](../../voyzu-core-concepts/control-accounts.md) explains the tax-to-GL mapping.

## Selecting the data

Search and filter by authority, component, document, date, filing period, or other available fields. Confirm the selected company and tax scope before comparing totals.

## Reading the detail

Open an entry to see its source document, tax rule or component, taxable base, tax amount, control account, filing context, reversal relationship, and linked journal where available.

## Source and scope

Tax entries are generated by posting documents under the company's country and tax profile. They are not manually edited tax adjustments.

## Refresh and export

Refresh before relying on recently posted activity. Export where the screen offers it, and record the selected company and filters with any output used as evidence.

## Corrections

This is an enquiry screen. Do not try to alter a ledger result here. Correct the source setup where appropriate, or post a supported correcting document or reversal so the financial and audit histories remain complete.
